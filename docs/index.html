A Bengali word-cross puzzle. Drag across the letters to spell a word; each word you find writes itself into the crossword above. Thirty-one levels, from three-letter consonant sets up to compound words like ফুলবাগান. Nothing to install, no sign-up, no ads — your progress is saved in this browser.

How to play

  • dragPress a letter and drag across the others. Sliding back onto the previous letter un-picks it.
  • clickPrefer clicking? Tap letters one at a time, then hit ✓ on the word — or press Enter. Esc clears.
  • ◈ 25A hint reveals one letter from a word you haven't solved. Words pay 5 coins per letter, plus 30 for clearing the level.

One tile = one akshara

মাছমা · ছvowel sign
বন্ধুব · ন্ধুconjunct
বাড়িবা · ড়িnukta + sign

Bengali isn't one letter per character — মাছ is three code points but two letter units. The game splits on those units, so a tile is always something a reader recognises.

Every board is generated from a word list when you open the level, not hand-drawn: words are placed by backtracking search so each one crosses another and no accidental word ever appears on the grid. Tiles hold aksharas — the letter units a Bengali reader sees — so মাছ is two tiles, মা and ছ, and the conjunct ন্ধু in বন্ধু is one. Bengali text is rendered with your system font. There is also an Android build of this game, sharing the same levels and the same layout code.
