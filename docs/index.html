Levels & guide

Levels - in teaching order

What this is

A Bengali word-cross puzzle, one letter of the alphabet at a time. Drag across the letters to spell a word; each word you find writes itself into the crossword above. Levels, and each one is a letter: the board for ক is কলম, কবুতর, কমলা, কমল, কম, কর, কল, কলা - eight words, all of them ক.

The alphabet opens with vowels, and those come first - অ আ, then ই উ, then এ ও, two to a level, because ঊ, এ and ঐ have barely a word each to their name. Then the consonants one at a time: ক, খ, গ, ঘ, চ, ছ, জ. ঙ and ঞ are skipped, since no Bengali word begins with either.

A few words on each board are there to hold the crossword together rather than for their letter - এক and ওজন share no letter at all, so something has to sit between them. More levels are on the way, picking up at ঝ. No word is ever set as a puzzle twice. Nothing to install, no sign-up, no ads - your progress is saved in this browser.

How to play

  • dragPress a letter and drag across the others. Sliding back onto the previous letter un-picks it.
  • clickPrefer clicking? Tap letters one at a time, then hit ✓ on the word - or press Enter. Esc clears.
  • finishClearing a level moves straight to the next one - no button, no waiting. The confetti plays across the change.
  • ‹ ›The arrows step to the previous or next level, and wrap around at either end. The level grid jumps straight to any level, and is coloured by stage of the syllabus. Any level you open starts blank, so an old one can always be played again.
  • soundEvery word is spoken as you find it. Bengali spelling hides the sound - ব and ভ, শ and ষ and স are each one sound but different letters - so hearing it is how it sticks. The control greys out on a device with no Bengali voice: silence beats an English voice reading Bengali.
  • ◈A hint reveals one letter from a word you haven't solved. It is free and there is no limit - there is no score in this game, and nothing to spend.

One tile = one akshara

মাছমা + ছvowel sign
বন্ধুব + ন্ধুconjunct
বাড়িবা + ড়িnukta + sign

Bengali isn't one letter per character - মাছ is three code points but two letter units. The game splits on those units, so a tile is always something a reader recognises.

Every board is generated from a word list when you open the level, not hand-drawn: words are placed by backtracking search so each one crosses another and no accidental word ever appears on the grid. Every board word is attested in real Bengali text - checked against corpus frequency data, then hand-picked from a vocabulary curated for a learner, because frequency alone offers verb inflections and newspaper register. No word is set as a puzzle twice, bar a few levels at the very start that have no other board available to them. Levels run in the order the writing system is taught rather than by puzzle difficulty alone, and each one records what it is the first to introduce. Tiles hold aksharas - the letter units a Bengali reader sees - so মাছ is two tiles, মা and ছ, and the conjunct ন্ধু in বন্ধু is one. Bengali text is rendered with the embedded font, and spoken with the device's own Bengali voice where there is one - recorded clips drop in over the top of that when they exist. There is also an Android build of this game, sharing the same levels and the same layout code, though pronunciation is web only so far.
