স্বরবর্ণ - the vowels

Eleven letters, and the order every Bengali child recites first. The game runs in this order, so these are levels 1 onward. Tap a letter to open its first level.

ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ - the consonants

Thirty-two letters, ক to হ. The game plays them in this order after the vowels - tap one to open its first level. Twenty-nine have levels; ঙ, ঞ and ণ have none and cannot, because no Bengali word begins with them. They still turn up inside words, so a child meets them - just never at the front, which is where a level's name comes from.

ড়, ঢ় and য় are usually counted here too, and the same is true of them: no word starts with one, and all three are on boards - বাড়ি, গাঢ়, নয়.

কার ও চিহ্ন - the other parts of a letter

A Bengali letter is rarely alone. A vowel sign - a কার - rides on it and changes the sound without being a letter of its own: ক becomes কা, কি, কু, কে, কো. That is why this game counts in aksharas rather than characters, and why কা is one tile and not two.

Each row below is a sign, the vowel it writes, and a word from the game that uses it. Tap the word to open the level it is on.

Levels - in teaching order

What this is

A Bengali word puzzle, one akshara at a time. Drag across the letters to spell a word; each word you find writes itself into the grid above. Levels, and each one is a single akshara: the board for কা is কাক, কাঠ, কান, কাচ, কাপ, কাঠি - six words, every one of them starting with কা.

That is the point of it. Bengali is taught as a letter and then the same letter under each vowel sign - ক, কা, কি, কু, কে, কো, কাঁ - and a level per akshara is a level per step of that series. A child who can read কাক can read কাঠ and কান without being taught them twice, and the level is where that clicks.

The levels run in the order of the বর্ণমালা, straight through. স্বরবর্ণ first - অ আ ই ঈ উ ঊ ঋ এ ঐ ও ঔ - then ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ, ক to হ. A letter carries as many levels as it has aksharas with words behind them: ক has sixteen, ঋ has one. Inside a letter the bare form leads and the rest follow the বারোখড়ি - ক কা কি কী কু কূ কৃ কে কৈ কো কৌ - so a child looking for ম finds it where ম belongs. ঙ, ঞ and ণ head no level, because no Bengali word begins with them, and neither do ড়, ঢ় or য় - all six still turn up inside words, just never at the front, which is where a level's name comes from.

Every sign of the alphabet is taught somewhere. Fifty-seven units - the eleven vowels, the thirty-two consonants ক to হ, the vowel signs and the conjunct families - and the last to arrive was ৈ, which writes ঐ onto a consonant. Bengali offers only four words carrying it, so three small levels hold all four: তৈরি, বৈশাখ, বৈঠক and সৈনিক.

Alphabet order has one honest cost, and it lands in the first quarter hour. Levels 12 and 14 are ঐ and ঔ, and word-initial ঐ and ঔ in Bengali are almost entirely abstract nouns under heavy conjuncts - ঐক্য, ঐতিহ্য, ঔদ্ধত্য, ঔজ্জ্বল্য. Those two levels teach the letter and nothing a child can picture, and there is no concrete alternative in the language to put there instead. The game is at its hardest early and then levels off rather than climbing. That is the price of being in alphabet order, and it is the order the game is for.

A board is not always one joined-up crossword. Words cross wherever they share a letter, but on a level where every word starts with the same akshara the only cell any two can share is that one - and a cell takes two words, not four. So a board is one island or two, and the words in each still cross.

Some aksharas come round twice - কা, then কা again straight after - which is more practice on the same step rather than a new one. Every word is a concrete thing a picture could show, or a number, a colour or an everyday action; the handful that are not carry a note saying how to draw them.

Everything is Bangladeshi Bengali: পানি rather than জল, চাচা and ফুফু rather than কাকা and পিসি. No word is ever set as a puzzle twice.

It works with no connection. Open it once and the whole game is on the device - all 256 levels, the words, the font. Your browser will offer to add it to the home screen, where it opens like an app and never asks for the network again. That is the point rather than a nicety: a data connection is something you have sometimes. No sign-up, no ads, nothing to pay - your progress is saved in this browser.

Found a problem?

A word spelled wrong, a word that is not the Bengali you use at home, a level that will not finish - that is worth telling someone, and it is usually a child's parent who notices.

There is no server behind this game and no account to sign into, which is deliberate and also means this button cannot send anything by itself. It copies your note instead, with the level you were on attached, and you paste it wherever suits you - WhatsApp, Messenger, email. Nothing leaves the device until you send it.

Every board is laid out when the game is built, not hand-drawn and not at runtime: every ordering of a level's words is tried and the smallest grid wins, so no accidental word ever appears on it. A wheel carries a letter twice when a word needs it twice, as ঘুঘু and বাবা do, and the two are kept apart on the ring so a drag from one to the other does not run over its twin. Every level is checked to be about its own akshara: a কা level is all কা words, and the build fails if one is not.

How to play

  • dragPress a letter and drag across the others. Sliding back onto the previous letter un-picks it.
  • clickPrefer clicking? Tap letters one at a time, then hit ✓ on the word - or press Enter. Esc clears.
  • finishClearing a level moves straight to the next one - no button, no waiting. The confetti plays across the change.
  • ‹ ›The arrows step to the previous or next level, and wrap around at either end. The level grid jumps straight to any level, and runs in alphabet order like the game. Any level you open starts blank, so an old one can always be played again.
  • ♪Every word is spoken aloud as you find it, and there is no button for it - hearing the word is the point, not a setting. Bengali spelling hides the sound: ব and ভ, শ and ষ and স are each one sound but different letters. On a device with no Bengali voice the game stays silent rather than reading Bengali in an English voice.
  • ◈A hint reveals one letter from a word you haven't solved. Free, and no limit - there is nothing to count and nothing to spend.
